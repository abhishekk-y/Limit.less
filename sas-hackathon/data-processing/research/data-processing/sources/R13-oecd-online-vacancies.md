# R13 — OECD online vacancy data as a partial indicator

- **Source:** OECD (2021), *An assessment of the impact of COVID-19 on job and skills demand using online job vacancy data*.
- **Type:** Official policy analysis.
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2021/04/an-assessment-of-the-impact-of-covid-19-on-job-and-skills-demand-using-online-job-vacancy-data_ad47b7c3/20fff09e-en.pdf
- **Credibility / recency / bias:** 5 / 3 / 2. Official analysis using a large commercial posting source; it concerns five OECD countries and a specific historical shock.
- **Relevant short excerpt:** “may not be representative of the overall vacancies being advertised.”
- **Finding used:** Online postings are granular and timely, but can overrepresent occupations/sectors; reweighting is not automatically justified and depends on reference data and purpose.
- **Design implication:** Label the two challenge job files separately; report source sample and subgroup coverage before considering any weighting.
- **Limit:** International estimates are not an India validation and should not be transferred as numeric benchmarks.
