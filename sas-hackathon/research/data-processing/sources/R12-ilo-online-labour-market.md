# R12 — ILO online labour-market data methods

- **Source:** Fabo, B. & Kureková, L. M. (2022), *Methodological issues related to the use of online labour market data*, ILO Working Paper 68.
- **Type:** International organization working paper / methodological review.
- **URL:** https://www.ilo.org/publications/methodological-issues-related-use-online-labour-market-data
- **Credibility / recency / bias:** 5 / 3 / 2. Institutional methods synthesis; not a validation of this particular hackathon sample.
- **Relevant short excerpt:** “many empirical studies do not engage with the methodological aspects and weaknesses of online labour market data and take them at face value.”
- **Finding used:** Online job-posting and applicant sources can support bounded pattern discovery, but non-representativeness and source/structure fluctuations need explicit handling.
- **Design implication:** Use observed-sample language, make data coverage and source composition visible, and avoid extrapolating sample frequencies to all hiring.
- **Limit:** The paper synthesizes multiple countries/sources; its broad methods do not identify the selection process of the four challenge files.
