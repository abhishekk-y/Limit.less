# R19 — Evaluating analytical alternatives

- **Source:** Del Giudice, M. & Gangestad, S. W. (2021), *A Traveler's Guide to the Multiverse: Promises, Pitfalls, and a Framework for the Evaluation of Analytic Decisions*.
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed methods paper.
- **URL:** https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2515245920954925
- **Credibility / recency / bias:** 4 / 4 / 2. Methodological framework; illustrative examples are not a rule that all analyses need an exhaustive multiverse.
- **Relevant short excerpt:** “if specifications are not truly arbitrary, multiverse-style analyses can produce misleading results.”
- **Finding used:** Alternative specifications should be defensible and defined before looking for preferred results; nonequivalent measures should not be collapsed as though interchangeable.
- **Design implication:** Predeclare only the choices that represent genuine uncertainty (e.g., reviewed aliases; exact text duplication; validated weight cap), preserve failed specifications, and show why each choice is included.
- **Limit:** A few sensitivity scenarios are not full multiverse inference; they do not fix selection bias.
