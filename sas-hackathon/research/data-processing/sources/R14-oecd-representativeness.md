# R14 — OECD comparison of postings to national sources

- **Source:** OECD (2024), *How well do online job postings match national sources in large English-speaking countries?*.
- **Type:** OECD working paper / comparison against national statistical sources.
- **URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-well-do-online-job-postings-match-national-sources-in-large-english-speaking-countries_c17cae09-en.html
- **Credibility / recency / bias:** 5 / 4 / 2. Official comparative analysis; the countries studied are not India.
- **Finding used:** Compare online vacancy data with official sources across dimensions; representativeness varies by dimension and cannot be presumed.
- **Design implication:** Add external benchmark validation only when geography, occupation classifications, time, and measures match. Until then, keep claims inside the supplied samples.
- **Limit:** Not a general calibration factor for these files; no numbers should be imported into the project as a correction.
