# R17 — Weak supervision for job-skill extraction

- **Source:** Zhang et al. (2022), *Skill Extraction from Job Postings using Weak Supervision*.
- **Type:** Research paper / weakly supervised skill taxonomy method.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08071
- **Credibility / recency / bias:** 4 / 3 / 2. Technical paper; its method and label resources do not constitute performance evidence on the challenge data.
- **Finding used:** Taxonomies and weak supervision can reduce manual labeling burden while retaining a defined concept system.
- **Design implication:** Use a human-reviewed mapping table with canonical concept, source phrases, accepted variants, rejection reason and version. A model may assist candidate generation only after local validation and event-rule review.
- **Limit:** A literature method is not a substitute for a benchmark on the supplied job text.
