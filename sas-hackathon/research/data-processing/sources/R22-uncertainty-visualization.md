# R22 — Visual uncertainty can change interpretation

- **Source:** Hofman, J., Goldstein, D. G. & Hullman, J. (2020), *How visualizing inferential uncertainty can mislead readers about treatment effects in scientific results*, CHI.
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed visualization/HCI experiments.
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/how-visualizing-inferential-uncertainty-can-mislead-readers-about-treatment-effects-in-scientific-results/
- **Credibility / recency / bias:** 4 / 3 / 2. Controlled experiments; task and audience differ from this dashboard.
- **Finding used:** A visual interval’s meaning can affect judgments; estimate uncertainty and outcome variability are distinct.
- **Design implication:** Explain what a chart’s uncertainty represents, put denominators and caveats directly beside the estimate, and do not use an unexplained “confidence” badge.
- **Limit:** Results from experimental tasks do not prove that a specific Limit.less visualization improves decisions; usability evaluation remains needed.
