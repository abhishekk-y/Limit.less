# S07 — LLM-supervised multilingual skill extraction from job ads

- Authors: Wang and Sun; NLDB 2025 proceedings published 2026.
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-97144-0_9
- Type/assessment: peer-reviewed conference proceedings; high credibility and recent; large-scale and externally supervised design.
- Short evidence excerpt: the work uses “3M+ postings from 10k+ sources” and 500k+ sentences.
- Relevance: taxonomy-aligned sentence classification and probabilistic skill distributions are a sophisticated direction. Its scale is orders of magnitude above the hackathon files and its external LLM supervision is incompatible with the stated data boundary.
- Use in this project: take the idea of confidence-weighted skill distributions, but prototype only with local inference and human-audited labels. Do not imply the published model is trained or validated in Limit.less.
