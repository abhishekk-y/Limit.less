# S03 — ONS method for labor-demand volumes using online ads

- Publisher: UK Office for National Statistics, 2024 methodology guide.
- URL: https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/methodologies/measuringlabourdemandvolumesacrosstheukusingtextkerneldatauserguide
- Type/assessment: official statistics methodology; high credibility and recency; UK/vendor-specific.
- Short evidence excerpt: “a vacancy is a position for which an employer is actively seeking recruits”; an advert can differ.
- Relevance: ONS describes cleaning, geography assignment, duplicate removal, occupation classification, and distinct “new advert” vs “snapshot” metrics.
- Use in this project: keep ad counts distinct from vacancies, retain unknown geography, deduplicate conservatively, and only claim trends when repeated comparable snapshots exist. The UK method cannot be directly claimed as validated for India.
