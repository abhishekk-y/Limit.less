# S08 — Methodological issues in online labor-market data

- Publisher: International Labour Organization, Working Paper 68, 2022.
- URL: https://www.ilo.org/publications/methodological-issues-related-use-online-labour-market-data
- Type/assessment: intergovernmental research review; high credibility, medium recency; broad and heterogeneous settings.
- Short evidence excerpt: proposed responses include “adapting the research questions and research focus to the quality of data.”
- Relevance: online vacancy/applicant data can be useful but fluctuates and is not representative by default; mixed methods and statistical corrections are context-dependent.
- Use in this project: scope conclusions to the files and observed posting data. The result should be useful as a decision-support sample, not marketed as a census or causal model.
