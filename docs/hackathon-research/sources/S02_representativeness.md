# S02 — Assessing the representativeness of online job advertisements

- Publisher: Publications Office of the European Union, 2022.
- URL: https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/4e3beac2-8be3-11ed-999b-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
- Type/assessment: official statistical working paper; high credibility, medium recency; EU labor-market context.
- Short evidence excerpt: “none of the three sources allows precise estimates on the number of vacancies to be derived.”
- Relevance: the analysis benchmarks OJAs against labor-force and job-vacancy surveys, finding coverage differences across sector, occupation, and geography. Benchmark sources also have limitations.
- Use in this project: describe the supplied ads as a sample; do not call counts “all jobs,” a national census, or vacancies without qualifications. India-specific representativeness remains unknown.
