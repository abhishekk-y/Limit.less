# S01 — Skill Extraction from Job Postings using Weak Supervision

- Authors: Zhang, Jensen, van der Goot, Plank; RecSys in HR workshop, 2022.
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08071
- Type/assessment: peer-reviewed workshop paper; high credibility for research method, medium recency; English/ESCO domain limits transfer.
- Short evidence excerpt: “Precision goes up with a higher threshold, while recall goes down.”
- Relevance: taxonomy-guided representations can propose skill spans without first labeling every posting, but thresholds trade false positives against missed skills. The paper reports substantial performance differences between datasets, supporting local validation instead of importing published F1 as a Limit.less result.
- Use in this project: frozen local model may suggest candidate spans; a reviewed annotation sample remains necessary. This paper does not authorize using its model/data or sending challenge postings to an external service.
