# S06 — Prediction model evaluation and external validation

- Authors: Riley et al., BMJ 2024;384:e074819.
- URL: https://www.bmj.com/content/384/bmj-2023-074819.full
- Type/assessment: peer-reviewed methods review; high credibility and recency; clinical application context.
- Short evidence excerpt: bootstrap evaluates the “model building process itself” and estimates optimism.
- Relevance: validation must repeat the full modeling process; random holdout wastes scarce observations in small samples; external validation requires new relevant data. Calibration and discrimination both matter.
- Use in this project: a high internal score is not proof of generalization; no claim of 98% predictive accuracy is justified without a prespecified target, adequate sample size, and independent evaluation.
